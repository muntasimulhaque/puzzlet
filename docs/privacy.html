Puzzlet: Privacy Policy

Puzzlet does not collect, share, or transmit any data. Ever.

Puzzlet is a paid, offline jigsaw puzzle game for young children. It has no advertising, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no internet permission at all. The app cannot connect to the network, because the Android system gives it no way to.

What stays on your device

  • The sound on/off switch.
  • At most one unfinished puzzle (which picture it was, and which pieces were already placed), so your child can pause and return.

These preferences never leave your device. Uninstalling the app removes them permanently.

What we never do

  • We never collect personal data of any kind, from anyone.
  • We never show ads or promote other products.
  • We never send anything over the internet.
  • We never ask a child (or a parent) for a name, an email, an age, or a location.

Children

Puzzlet is designed for young children and complies with Google Play's Families policy. Because it collects nothing, transmits nothing, shows nothing but its own pictures, and links nowhere, it is safe by construction, not just by policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date. Given how the app is built, it is hard to imagine what could change.
