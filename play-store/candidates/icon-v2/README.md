# Icon v2 takes: point at one

Six rendered takes, all in the chosen palette (lagoon, paper, honey once)
and Baloo, all subjects inanimate. Generated by :tools:makeCandidates,
never hand edited. Nothing here ships until a take is chosen.

Icons (512 tile):
- icon-A-four-outie: current idea kept, fixed properly. Mushroom knobs
  with thick necks, soft contact shadow, top light, calm gradient tile.
- icon-B-interlock (my recommendation): classic piece, top and bottom
  outie, left and right innie. Reads as a puzzle at any size, still
  symmetric and calm. Speaks for itself.
- icon-C-click: two pieces about to click. Likely too small and busy
  at 48 dp. Included so you can reject it with eyes, not prose.
- icon-D-board: literal 2 by 2 board with one piece lifted. Likely too
  busy for an icon, but it may suit the feature graphic. Same deal.

Feature (1024 by 500):
- feature-B-calm: big interlock piece, clean wordmark, honey rule,
  no lattice behind the words.
- feature-D-play: the play moment, mini board left, words right.
