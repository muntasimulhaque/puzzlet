# Icon v3 takes: from scratch, point at one

Three new directions drawn from what the game actually is. Same palette,
no text, nothing alive. Generated by :tools:makeCandidatesV3, never hand
edited. Shipped icons stay pinned until one wins. See sheet-all.png for
all three at tile size plus true 48 px rows.

- icon-H-hover: the snap about to happen. One honest piece floats above
  its own slot, larger because nearer, its shadow falling into the slot.
  Tray, drag, snap in one image. Calm and symmetric.
- icon-S-seam: two pieces cropped by the frame, paper against deep,
  divided only by their interlock curve. Macro and two tone. Boldest at
  small sizes, least literal as a puzzle.
- icon-W-well: the board itself. Paper board, one empty slot, one piece
  just lifted with its shadow on the board. The whole game in one tile.
  Most literal, busiest at 48 dp.

Reply with the take name (for example: H) plus any single change. The
winner gets rebuilt across all densities plus adaptive and monochrome,
with pins, in MakeIcons.

Outcome: S won, with a socket added above the knob. Finals live in
MakeIcons (all densities plus adaptive and monochrome, probed) and
MakeArt (S tile feature with clean type). This sheet stays as the
decision record.
